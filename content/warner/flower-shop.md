title: Petals

There was a beautiful flower shop a few blocks from here. She used to drag her there after work. The owner was a small woman, very small indeed, and she had the blackest hair with streaks of gray running through it like veins of graphite. The smallest voice, as well, with a rolling, waving accent that was full of care.

She hated her.

But Zahra did not know that and did not need to know that.

I should visit that shop. She would love to breathe the ashes and smell her bones.
