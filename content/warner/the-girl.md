title: Faces

They didn’t like either of her new [[mask-of-the-raven|faces]]. The white porcelain shell nor the callous grub that writhed underneath. The eyes, they said, were black and soulless and therefore less human. As if that had been a concern of hers. But the eyes that cowered behind them were even less so, and she believed that they could sense that.

There was a girl she once knew.

The girl would never remember this, but she said the mask was beautiful. Not to suggest what lay beneath was any less so, but there was a charm to it, the girl said. In the way it forced her to smile even as she scowled. Made her watch as she winced. Breathe as she choked.

It was hopeful, the girl said.

The girl said a whole lot of things. Things about pieces of her anatomy other than her face. Things about her gait back when she had one. The girl said that she walked as though she was treading through breast-high tides of ocean water, and talked as though she had lungs full of that same water. The girl was right.

But the girl would not remember those things, {{focus|were you to ask her}}.
