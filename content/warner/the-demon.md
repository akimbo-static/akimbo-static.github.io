title: Lance

The Demon told me that she deserved it. This was no matter of debate. If I had eyes to see, and soul to accept–I, too, could have witnessed the wonders with which he is vested.

The Demon was a creature of many wonders.

The Demon told me to reject uncertainty. It said that time and matter were determinate. That the happenings of now were those of tomorrow, and the train always ran its course no matter the passengers.

This is my stop. I should get off while I can.

The Demon said many things. I prompted it, after all, shouldn’t I feel responsible? Shouldn’t I feel ashamed? Shouldn’t I be grovelling in the dust begging for–

I am ashamed. She can see that better than I can.

The Demon told me that she would bleed. That there was nothing I or anyone else could do, that our dimension of influence was simply… on a lower plane.

She would bleed. And I would accept. That was a certainty.

The Demon was never wrong.
