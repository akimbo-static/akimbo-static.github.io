title: Insertion

The walls of eternal darkness are disturbed as I enter.

There is a ripple, unseen but felt, invisible but with the power of a shockwave, which emanates from my body.

It is cold. I feel nothing.

There is no air. Only the dust of pulverized bones and the black space around me and its infinite weight crushing my chest.

The city greets me. Voices all around. Whispers. They are glad in their souls to witness, have watched ages pass waiting for my arrival, and now step aside for my clear passage into the abyss.

The walls croak and rubble scatters across my bloody face.

It is hot. I feel {{focus|everything}}.
