title: Ritual Blade

A sharpened blade rusted with the blood of previous users.

Hopelessness is endemic among the Wretched Dead, and to them the prospect of True Death is a comfort.

The Strix are willing cooperators in the wreaking of havoc, and a blood sacrifice is sure to prove your sincerity. Only be wary of the debt you might accrue from accepting such questionable aid.
