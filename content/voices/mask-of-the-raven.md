title: Mask of the Raven

Loose-fitting mask of charred flesh, grants one the rage of a dying {{wisp|soul}}.

The lips are missing. They say that she kept them, for purposes none too saintly.

The rest, she tore from her head and left in the darkness with us.

Though she entered my realm for but an instant, it felt as if aeons were dragging by. I could feel her pain as though it were my own. Behind her eyes lived horrors that could not be found in the darkest pits of hell, and with every moment threatened to burst forth like a {{wisp|fountain of black blood}}.

Then she was gone. The Raven lived, and left her horrid creations behind to torment the Dead.
