title: Burnt Photograph

I have indeed heard that objects of consequence could follow one through the veil, though had not confirmed it till now.

Though I suppose, you haven’t come naked and therefore it seems self-evident.

She is beautiful. Rarely have I seen such a strong countenance from one so far gone, even as her memories of agony hit her in waves of torment, her jaw remained set and her eyes glossy black orbs of resolve. Upon seeing her living self, it seems she has always worn that particular mask.

I only wonder if they have yet found a way to remove it.
