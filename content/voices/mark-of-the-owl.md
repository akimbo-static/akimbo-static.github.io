title: Mark of the Owl

A brand, burned into the skin of every Trespasser upon entering the City.

All of your kind are one and the same, meddlers who invade and disrupt that which you do not understand. There are powers at play in this rotten cesspool which you could scarcely imagine, forces of eternity ripping and tearing apart the very fabric of existence.

Yet your mortality is of such vital importance to you.

For some, the stakes are even more trifling. You shall see, in due time, the depths of the Owl’s depravity.
